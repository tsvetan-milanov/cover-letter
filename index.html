I'm a Product Manager who likes making complex technology simple. I try to understand a problem from the ground up, break it into its core parts, and find the smallest solution that still delivers real value.

Over the past five years at Chaos, I've worked on platform products, AI features, semantic search, and analytics, helping build products that architects, designers, and 3D artists use every day. Lately AI has become a core part of how I work, allowing me to prototype ideas, automate workflows, and test solutions with working software instead of docs and slides.

I believe good products don't ask users to understand the system - the system should just feel natural.

Product Philosophy

I believe the best products hide complexity instead of showing it off. I start by understanding the real problem, build the simplest version that solves it, and test it as early as I can. AI hasn't changed how I think about products, but has surely changed how fast I can turn thinking into something real, so I can learn and adjust as I go.
